### What's a smartphone?

BEFORE (original model):
A **smartphone** is a mobile phone that offers advanced computing power and connectivity, far exceeding the capabilities of traditional "dumb" phones (which were primarily used for voice calls and basic texting).

### Key Characteristics:
1. **Touchscreen Interface**: Almost all modern smartphones use capacitive touchscreens as their primary input method, replacing physical keypads.
2. **Operating System (OS)**: They run a full-fledged operating system (like **iOS** for iPhones, **Android** for most other brands, or historically Windows Phone/Symbian), allowing users to install and run applications.
3. **Internet Connectivity**: Equipped with Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, and cellular data (3G/4G/5G) for seamless web browsing, email, and cloud services.
4. **Multimedia Capabilities**: High-quality cameras, video recording, music playback, and video streaming.
5. **App Ecosystem**: Access to app stores (e.g., Apple App Store, Google Play Store) with millions of applications for communication, productivity, entertainment, navigation, and more.
6. **Sensors**: Include GPS, accelerometers, gyroscopes, proximity sensors, and sometimes biometric sensors (fingerprint or face recognition) for security and functionality.

### Common Uses:
- Voice calls and texting
- Web browsing and email
- Social media
- Photography and videography
- Navigation and maps
- Mobile banking and payments
- Streaming music and video
- Gaming
- Productivity (documents, calendars, notes)

### Popular Brands:
- **Apple** (iPhone)
- **Samsung** (Galaxy series)
- **Xiaomi**
- **Google** (Pixel series)
- **OnePlus**
- **Huawei** (in many markets)

In essence, a smartphone is a handheld computer with a phone number, capable of running a wide variety of software and connecting to the global internet.

### What's TikTok?

BEFORE (original model):
**TikTok** is a popular social media platform that allows users to create and share short-form video content. Launched in 2017 as a standalone app (after merging with Musical.ly), it has rapidly become one of the most widely used apps globally.

### Key Features:
1. **Short-Form Videos**: Most videos are between 15 seconds and 10 minutes long, making them easy to consume and share.
2. **Algorithm-Driven Feed**: Unlike traditional social media that relies heavily on follower counts, TikTok uses a sophisticated algorithm to recommend content based on user behavior, interests, and engagement—meaning even new accounts with few followers can go viral.
3. **Creative Tools**: Users can add music, sound effects, filters, duets, stitches, and text overlays to enhance their videos.
4. **Community & Trends**: The platform thrives on trends, challenges, and memes, encouraging user participation and creativity.

### Why It’s Popular:
- **Entertainment**: Offers a wide range of content, including comedy, dance, lip-syncs, tutorials, storytelling, and educational videos.
- **Accessibility**: Easy to use and highly engaging, especially among younger audiences (Gen Z and Millennials), though it has a broad user base.
- **Creator Economy**: Many creators use TikTok to build audiences, promote businesses, and even earn money through brand partnerships and in-app gifting.

### Controversies & Concerns:
- **Privacy**: There have been concerns about data collection and privacy, particularly regarding its Chinese parent company, ByteDance.
- **Regulation**: TikTok has faced scrutiny and bans in some countries (e.g., the U.S. and India) over national security and data privacy issues.
- **Addictive Design**: Its endless scroll and algorithm are designed to keep users engaged for long periods, raising concerns about screen time and mental health.

### In Summary:
TikTok is a leading social media platform known for its creative, algorithm-driven short videos that appeal to a global audience. It has reshaped how people consume and create digital content, making it a significant player in the social media landscape.
