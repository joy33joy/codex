### What's a smartphone?

BEFORE (original model):
A **smartphone** is a mobile phone that runs an operating system and allows users to install and run a variety of applications, much like a small computer.

Unlike basic "feature phones" that are limited to calling and texting, smartphones offer a wide range of advanced capabilities, including:

### Key Features:
1. **Touchscreen Interface**: Most modern smartphones use capacitive touchscreens for input.
2. **Internet Access**: Built-in Wi-Fi and cellular data (4G/5G) allow for browsing the web, email, and cloud services.
3. **App Ecosystem**: Users can download apps from app stores (like the Apple App Store or Google Play Store) for social media, gaming, productivity, navigation, and more.
4. **Multimedia Capabilities**:
   - High-quality cameras for photos and videos.
   - Media players for music, streaming video, and podcasts.
5. **Connectivity**: Supports Bluetooth, NFC, GPS, and various wireless protocols.
6. **Operating Systems**: Run on sophisticated OSes such as:
   - **iOS** (Apple iPhones)
   - **Android** (Samsung, Google Pixel, OnePlus, etc.)
   - Less common: HarmonyOS, KaiOS, etc.

### How It Differs from a Basic Phone:
| Feature | Basic Phone | Smartphone |
|--------|-------------|------------|
| Primary Use | Calls, SMS | Calls, SMS + Internet, Apps, Media |
| Screen | Small, non-touch or basic touch | Large, high-resolution touchscreen |
| Processing Power | Limited | Powerful CPU/GPU |
| Storage | Minimal | Large internal + expandable storage |
| Camera | Basic | Advanced with multiple lenses, AI features |

### Common Uses:
- Communication (calls, texts, video chats)
- Social media
- Web browsing
- Photography and videography
- Navigation and mapping
- Mobile banking and payments
- Gaming
- Productivity (email, documents, calendars)

In essence, a smartphone is a pocket-sized computer that has become a central part of daily life for most people worldwide.
